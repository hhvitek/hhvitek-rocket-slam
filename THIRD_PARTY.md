# Third-party assets

Policy for Rocket Slam:

- **CC0 only.** No attribution-required, non-commercial or "free for personal use" licences.
- **Official sources only:** [kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl), [polyhaven.com](https://polyhaven.com),
  [ambientcg.com](https://ambientcg.com). No mirrors or re-uploads.
- Stored in `unity/Assets/ThirdParty/<source>/` (`Kenney/`, `PolyHaven/`, `ambientCG/`).
- Every asset gets a row below before it is committed.

Unity packages (from the Unity Package Manager) and the URP template settings assets are part of
the engine, not third-party art, and are not listed here. All 3D models are generated by our own
Blender scripts in `blender/scripts/`.
